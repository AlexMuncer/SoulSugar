Frequently Asked Questions

HOW DO I BOOK SOUL SUGAR? +
Booking is super easy! We’ll guide you through every step. To lock in your date, all we need is a 20% deposit and a signed booking contract. The rest isn’t due until a week before your event – simple as that!
HOW FAR DO YOU TRAVEL? +
We’re based in Liverpool and travel free of charge to events within an hour.But we’re always up for a road trip and regularly perform at weddings and events all over the UK!
WILL YOU PLAY MY FIRST DANCE/CAN I MAKE REQUESTS? +
Absolutely! Soul Sugar are more than happy to accommodate your first dance and song requests. Just get in touch to chat through the details.
HOW LONG DO YOU PLAY FOR? +
Soul Sugar tailor their sets to each event to ensure the perfect experience for you and your guests. Generally speaking, We've found two 45-minute to 1 hour sets work best, but we’re happy to work with you on the timing to suit your event’s unique needs. Just let us know what you're looking for, and we'll make it happen!
DO YOU HAVE A DJ SERVICE? +
Soul Sugar don’t provide a live DJ, but we’ll play a personalised playlist curated by you through our PA system until the end of the event, at no extra charge.
WHEN DO I NEED TO BOOK? +
Soul Sugar are in high demand (for good reason!), so we recommend booking as early as you can to secure your date.
HOW LONG DO SOUL SUGAR NEED TO SET UP? +
Soul Sugar usually allow around an hour for set-up and soundcheck.
